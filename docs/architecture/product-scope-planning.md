# Product scope planning

The planner selects the smallest profile supported by approved requirements:

- `marketing-site`: public informational pages with minimal interaction;
- `business-site`: public business pages with structured forms or business data;
- `web-application`: approved authentication, roles, dashboards, persistence, uploads, or stateful workflows.

The product scope lists outcomes, approved capabilities, explicit exclusions, roles, entities, workflows, constraints, and acceptance mapping. Recommendations remain separate and cannot become implementation scope without approval.
