# Design directions

Each direction describes concept, business and audience fit, mood, semantic color roles, typography, layout, grid, hero, navigation, section rhythm, components, forms, CTAs, image art direction, logo rules, motion, responsive principles, anti-template rules, advantages, risks, complexity, and requirement/planning references.

The three directions must differ strategically in composition, typography, rhythm, density, imagery, component character, hero approach, and interaction emphasis. Color-only variations are rejected.
