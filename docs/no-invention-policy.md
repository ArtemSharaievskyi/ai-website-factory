# No-invention policy

User-stated facts and confirmed answers are evidence. Recommendations are labeled separately and never silently become requirements. The Lead Agent must ask for unverifiable business facts such as testimonials, certifications, licenses, operating hours, guarantees, VAT details, or phone numbers rather than supplying plausible values.

Images require an explicit source decision. A user-supplied logo is referenced by metadata; the Factory does not generate logos. Placeholder imagery requires an explicit user decision and remains a brief requirement, not an implicit asset.

Fixed-stack choices are architecture constraints, not clarification questions. The Lead Agent records decisions relevant to the brief but does not add infrastructure or change the fixed stack.
